Website: the public read-only version lives at marketing-trls.github.io. To update it, open Caption pack, click Export for website, then upload posts.json to the data folder of the marketing-trls.github.io repository on GitHub.

How it works: upload photos and videos to the Drive folder. The planner checks the folder when you open it and tells you about new, revised or removed files. New files get the next free posting time and hook, body and call-to-action captions for every platform, written by Claude in the Tara Rose voice and optimised for SEO, AEO, GEO and AI answers. Fill in the specialist, branch and keyword on a post, then click Audit & optimise to run your audit prompt. Captions use your own Claude plan; the first time, Claude asks you to allow it.
